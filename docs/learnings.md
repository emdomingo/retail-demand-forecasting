**Causal Inference**
Forecasting answers: How much will sell?
Causal Half: "When an event happens (e.g. price cut), how much of the sales bump was caused by that event, vs it happening anyways?

Tool for isolating the causal-piece: "Difference-in-Differences (DID)"
